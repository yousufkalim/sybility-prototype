Common Controls are security controls that are implemented to support multiple information resources.

When Risk Managers work to assess resources independently, assessment of common controls may result in duplication of effort and considerable extra work by control owners and Risk Managers alike.

Sibylity allows Risk Managers assessing resource-specific security controls to inherit the status of controls that are not resource-specific from Groups that make such information available. To share the status of common controls offered by your Group, update this assessment to reflect that status. When you are ready, select to share your assessment. It will be made available to other Risk Managers in your organization for their use in their own assessments.
